# CivicFlow — Smart Business Support Platform

CivicFlow is a smart, user-friendly business support platform designed to help entrepreneurs, startups, and small business owners explore government schemes, organize compliance tasks, and discover useful business resources in one place.

The platform provides a centralized dashboard that simplifies access to business-related information and helps users navigate important steps involved in starting and managing a business.

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Government Scheme Discovery:** Explore government initiatives and business support programs, including MSME-related schemes and startup resources.
- **Compliance Task Tracker:** Organize important business compliance activities and keep track of pending tasks.
- **Business Registration Resources:** Discover information about business registration, Udyam registration, GST, and related services.
- **Startup Support:** Explore resources and initiatives designed to support entrepreneurs and emerging businesses.
- **Advisor Discovery:** Browse sample business advisor profiles to explore potential guidance and support.
- **Application Workspace:** Use a centralized interface to organize and monitor application-related information.
- **Interactive Dashboard:** Navigate business resources through a simple, organized, and user-friendly interface.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of CivicFlow is to make business support information easier to discover and organize. By bringing useful resources, compliance tracking, and business support features together, the platform aims to provide entrepreneurs with a convenient starting point for managing their business journey.
